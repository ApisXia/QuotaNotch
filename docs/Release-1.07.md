# QuotaNotch 1.07

刘海里的额度圆环换成了像素宠物。宠物身上的计量就是剩余额度，同时对任务做出反应；刘海大小可以分三档调整。

- **像素宠物：** 可选麻薯（猫）、比特（机器人）、果冻（史莱姆）。猫的饭碗、机器人的胸口进度条、史莱姆的水位显示剩余额度；额度偏低时变黄、变红，用得偏快时睁大眼睛，用完会睡着，额度恢复时开心一下。配色可保持原色或按 Claude / Codex 着色。
- **任务一眼可见：** 没有音乐时，宠物右边是剩余百分比、进度条和最多三个任务圆点（绿色运行中、琥珀色闪烁为等你批准、蓝色已完成）；播放音乐时，左边是封面，任务显示在宠物右下角的小标签上。宠物会抬头拍玻璃提醒你批准，任务完成后开心几秒。运行时宠物周围有淡淡的波纹，等你批准时会闪烁，可在「柔光」和「像素」两种效果之间切换。
- **点击即达：** 有任务显示时，点刘海直接打开任务页；没有任务时打开额度页。
- **音乐时的额度提示：** 播放音乐时，额度跌破 50%、20%、用完、恢复或用得偏快，刘海下方会短暂显示一行，例如「Claude 5 小时 · 剩余 18% · 2 小时后重置」。
- **三档大小：** 「大字模式」换成滑块。标准只放大宠物；小和大会一起调整刘海里的图标和展开后的面板。
- **更稳的布局：** 刘海宽度不再随任务出现或消失跳动，摄像头始终居中；展开动画从中心张开，不再往左偏。额度面板恢复为 Claude / Codex 分标签显示。
- **移除：** 额度圆环模式、收起时左右滑动切换额度与任务，以及走路的小猫。展开后顶部整行左右滑动切页仍然保留。

**安装：** 先退出 QuotaNotch，下载 `QuotaNotch-1.07.dmg`，将应用拖入「应用程序」替换。支持 Apple Silicon 与 Intel，要求 macOS 15 或更新版本。应用版本为 **1.07（310）**。安装包沿用临时签名，尚未进行 Apple 公证；更新后可能需要重新授予辅助功能权限。

## English

The quota ring in the closed notch is now a pixel pet. Its body shows your remaining quota, and it reacts to your tasks. The notch also comes in three sizes.

- **Pixel pets:** Choose Mochi (cat), Bit (robot) or Jelly (slime). The cat's bowl, the robot's chest meter and the slime's water level show remaining quota. They turn yellow, then red, when quota runs low, look alarmed when you are using it too fast, fall asleep when it is empty and celebrate when it refills. Keep their own colors or tint them by Claude / Codex.
- **Tasks at a glance:** Without music, the remaining percentage, a quota bar and up to three task dots sit beside the pet (green running, blinking amber needs you, blue done). While music plays, the album takes the left side and tasks show as a small tag on the pet. The pet taps the glass when a task needs you and celebrates briefly when one finishes. A faint ripple surrounds it while tasks run and it blinks when one needs you, in your choice of Soft glow or Pixel effects.
- **One tap away:** While tasks are shown, clicking the notch opens Tasks; otherwise it opens Quota.
- **Quota peeks during music:** Crossing 50%, 20% or empty, a refill, or a too-fast pace shows a short line below the notch, such as "Claude 5h · 18% left · resets in 2h".
- **Three sizes:** The larger-text option is now a slider. Standard enlarges only the pet; Small and Large also resize the notch icons and the opened panel.
- **Steadier layout:** The notch no longer changes width as tasks come and go, the camera stays centered, and the panel opens from the center instead of drifting left. The quota panel is back to one tab per service.
- **Removed:** The quota ring mode, swiping between quota and tasks in the closed notch, and the walking cat. Swiping across the opened header to change pages is kept.

**Install:** Quit QuotaNotch, download `QuotaNotch-1.07.dmg`, and replace the app in Applications. macOS 15+, Apple Silicon and Intel. App version: **1.07 (310)**. The app remains ad-hoc signed and is not Apple-notarized; you may need to grant Accessibility again after updating.
