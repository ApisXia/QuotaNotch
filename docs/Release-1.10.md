# QuotaNotch 1.10

正在播放更稳、封面更全；日程里的会议可以一键加入，还有周视图；Dock 里不再出现图标。

- **正在播放更稳：** 读取播放信息的组件意外退出后会自己重启，不用重开应用。
- **封面更全：** 歌曲没有封面时（QQ 音乐、部分浏览器、Apple Music 的在线歌曲）会自动查找，中文歌会在中文商店里找。切歌时不再闪过上一首的封面，封面也更清晰。
- **来源图标：** 浏览器里播放时显示浏览器的图标。设置里可以隐藏封面角上的来源应用小图标。
- **一键加入会议：** 点一下带会议链接的日程就能加入，支持 Zoom、Google Meet、Teams、Webex、Whereby、Jitsi、腾讯会议、飞书和钉钉；右键可以复制链接或在“日历”中打开。
- **周视图：** 日历可以切换成周视图，并选择一周从哪天开始。全天提醒事项显示在正确的日期。
- **没有 Dock 图标：** 打开设置或任务监控时 Dock 里也不再出现图标。从“应用程序”里再次打开 QuotaNotch 会直接打开设置。

**安装：** 1.08 及以后版本可以在应用内直接更新。也可以退出 QuotaNotch，下载 `QuotaNotch-1.10.dmg`，将应用拖入「应用程序」替换。支持 Apple Silicon 与 Intel，要求 macOS 15 或更新版本。应用版本为 **1.10（320）**。安装包沿用临时签名，尚未进行 Apple 公证。

## English

Steadier Now Playing with more covers, one-click meeting links and a week view in the calendar, and no Dock icon.

- **Steadier Now Playing:** If the part that reads playback stops, it restarts on its own; no need to reopen the app.
- **More covers:** When a song has no cover (QQ Music, some browsers, streamed Apple Music tracks), QuotaNotch looks one up, in the Chinese stores for Chinese titles. Changing songs no longer flashes the previous cover, and covers are sharper.
- **Source icon:** Playback in a browser shows the browser's icon. The small app icon on the cover can be hidden in Settings.
- **Join meetings in one click:** Click an event with a meeting link to join: Zoom, Google Meet, Teams, Webex, Whereby, Jitsi, Tencent Meeting, Feishu and DingTalk. Right-click to copy the link or open the event in Calendar.
- **Week view:** The calendar can show a week, starting on the day you choose. All-day reminders appear on the right day.
- **No Dock icon:** Opening Settings or the task monitor no longer puts an icon in the Dock. Opening QuotaNotch again from Applications opens Settings.

**Install:** 1.08 and later can update from inside the app. Or quit QuotaNotch, download `QuotaNotch-1.10.dmg`, and replace the app in Applications. macOS 15+, Apple Silicon and Intel. App version: **1.10 (320)**. The app remains ad-hoc signed and is not Apple-notarized.
