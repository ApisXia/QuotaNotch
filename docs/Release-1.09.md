# QuotaNotch 1.09

四只新宠物，每只宠物都更有自己的性格；设置里可以直接看着宠物挑；节日会戴上小帽子。

- **四只新宠物：** 摸鱼（鱼缸里的水就是额度）、鸭梨（额度越少，头上顶的山越大）、芭比Q了（额度越少烤得越焦，用完就“芭比Q了”）、牛马（用掉的额度变成车上的箱子）。每只点一下、连点、睡着时点都有自己的反应。
- **宠物用自己的话说：** 展开后宠物下面那行字换成它自己的话，比如“茶快见底了”“鸭梨山大”“七分熟”；数字和重置时间在下一行。
- **任务完成时各有各的庆祝：** 麻薯的茶冒出小心心，比特在屏幕上打勾，果冻高高蹦起，飘飘翻个跟头，苗苗开花，鱼吐个大泡泡，鸭梨把山扔掉，烤肠被咬一口，牛马把货卸下车。
- **闲着时的小动作：** 什么都没发生时，宠物大约每 45 秒自己动一下：伸懒腰、屏保、抖一抖、躲起来、绕缸游一圈、嚼草……打开“减少动态效果”时不动。
- **看着挑宠物：** 设置里的宠物改成 9 张会动的小卡片，每张写着它怎么表示额度；刘海里齿轮菜单的每只宠物前面也有小图标。
- **节日装扮：** 春节（除夕到初七）、中秋前后、万圣节、圣诞会自动戴上小帽子，旁边还有灯笼、月亮、小蝙蝠、雪花；在设置里填上生日，那天会戴派对帽。设置里可以关掉。

**安装：** 1.08 可以在应用内直接更新。也可以退出 QuotaNotch，下载 `QuotaNotch-1.09.dmg`，将应用拖入「应用程序」替换。支持 Apple Silicon 与 Intel，要求 macOS 15 或更新版本。应用版本为 **1.09（315）**。安装包沿用临时签名，尚未进行 Apple 公证。

## English

Four new pets, more personality for every pet, a picker that shows the pets themselves, and holiday hats.

- **Four new pets:** Slacker (the water in its bowl is your quota), Duck Pear (the less quota, the bigger the mountain on its head), BBQ'd (the less quota, the more burnt; at zero it's BBQ'd), and Workhorse (used quota piles up as boxes on its cart). Each reacts in its own way to a click, to repeated clicks and to a click while it sleeps.
- **Pets speak for themselves:** In the opened notch, the line under the pet is in its own words, such as "Almost out of tea", "Crushing pressure" or "Well done". The numbers and reset time are on the line below.
- **A celebration of its own when a task finishes:** Mochi's tea steams little hearts, Bit puts a tick on its screen, Jelly bounces high, Wisp does a loop, Sprout blooms, Slacker blows a big bubble, Duck Pear tosses its mountain, BBQ'd gets a bite taken out, and Workhorse unloads its cart.
- **Small moves while nothing happens:** When nothing is going on, the pet does something small about every 45 seconds: a stretch, a screensaver, a jiggle, hiding, a lap of the bowl, chewing grass. Nothing moves with Reduce Motion on.
- **Pick a pet by looking at it:** Settings now shows the nine pets as small moving cards, each saying how it shows quota. The gear menu in the notch shows a small picture beside each pet.
- **Holiday outfits:** Pets wear a small hat for Lunar New Year (New Year's Eve to the 7th day), Mid-Autumn, Halloween and Christmas, with a lantern, a moon, a bat or snow beside them. Set your birthday in Settings for a party hat that day. Outfits can be turned off in Settings.

**Install:** 1.08 can update from inside the app. Or quit QuotaNotch, download `QuotaNotch-1.09.dmg`, and replace the app in Applications. macOS 15+, Apple Silicon and Intel. App version: **1.09 (315)**. The app remains ad-hoc signed and is not Apple-notarized.
