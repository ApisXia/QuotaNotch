# QuotaNotch 1.08

新增两只宠物，老三只重新画过；展开后宠物住在面板左边；从这一版开始可以在应用内检查更新。

- **新宠物：** 飘飘（小幽灵）越透明说明额度用得越多；苗苗（豆芽）的叶子随额度减少慢慢耷拉，花盆上的水位就是剩余额度。
- **老三只重新画过：** 麻薯旁边换成一大杯茶，茶的高度就是剩余额度，有余额时冒热气，嘴换成带小虎牙的 ω 嘴；比特肚子加宽，电量条变成 8 格；果冻换了一双亮晶晶的大眼睛。五只宠物都站在格子正中，波纹和光圈围着身体转。
- **展开后宠物在左边：** 任务页和额度页左边是一只放大的宠物和一行状态，例如「2 个在等你」「剩 64%」。有任务在等你时，点宠物直接打开那个任务；没有任务时它会时不时打个盹。任务列表每一行前面显示 Claude / Codex 图标，任务圆点的颜色和列表一致。
- **应用内更新：** 设置的「关于」里可以检查更新，也可以选择自动检查、自动下载并安装。每个更新包都会先校验签名再安装。
- **更清楚的提示效果：** 任务运行时的光圈和像素波纹更明显，波纹扩散得更远；宠物按 Claude / Codex 着色时，光圈和波纹也换成对应的颜色。
- **刘海更稳：** 刘海左右伸出的宽度只跟大小设置有关，开始或停止播放音乐时不再变宽变窄；封面和宠物一样大、在同一条中线上。小尺寸下百分比不再被截断。
- **展开动画：** 打开时刘海上的内容先向内收起，面板再展开；关闭时反过来。

**安装：** 先退出 QuotaNotch，下载 `QuotaNotch-1.08.dmg`，将应用拖入「应用程序」替换。支持 Apple Silicon 与 Intel，要求 macOS 15 或更新版本。应用版本为 **1.08（311）**。安装包沿用临时签名，尚未进行 Apple 公证；更新后可能需要重新授予辅助功能权限。以后的版本可以直接在应用内更新。

## English

Two new pets join, and the original three are redrawn. The opened notch now has the pet beside your tasks and quota, and the app can update itself from this version on.

- **New pets:** Wisp the ghost fades as you use your quota. Sprout's leaves droop as quota runs low, and the water band on its pot shows what is left.
- **Redrawn pets:** Mochi now has a tall cup of tea whose level is your remaining quota, steaming while there is quota left, and a ω mouth with one little fang. Bit has a wider belly with an 8-cell meter. Jelly has big shiny eyes. All five pets sit in the middle of their space, so the glow and ripples surround their bodies.
- **The pet in the opened notch:** Tasks and Quota show a larger pet on the left with one line of status, such as "2 need you" or "64% left". Click the pet to open the task that needs you; with nothing to report it dozes off now and then. Each task row starts with its Claude or Codex icon, and the task dots use the task list's colors.
- **In-app updates:** Check for updates in Settings › About, or let QuotaNotch check, download and install them automatically. Every update's signature is verified before it is installed.
- **Clearer effects:** The glow and pixel ripples around running tasks are easier to see, and the ripples travel farther. When pets are tinted by Claude / Codex, the glow and ripples take that color too.
- **A steadier notch:** How far the notch reaches to each side depends only on the size setting; it no longer widens or narrows when music starts or stops. The album cover is the same size as the pet and lines up with it. The percentage no longer gets cut off at the small size.
- **Opening and closing:** When the notch opens, its contents fold in toward the camera before the panel appears, and the reverse when it closes.

**Install:** Quit QuotaNotch, download `QuotaNotch-1.08.dmg`, and replace the app in Applications. macOS 15+, Apple Silicon and Intel. App version: **1.08 (311)**. The app remains ad-hoc signed and is not Apple-notarized; you may need to grant Accessibility again after updating. Later versions can be installed from inside the app.
